INKOS

E-Ink images, drawn in your browser

Black-and-white and six-colour pictures for E-Ink screens.

Notes, to-do lists, journals, logs, tables, charts and diagrams, written as a drawing program by a small model that runs on your device, then printed as a pure 1-bit or six-colour PNG. The Pro models paint manga and illustration pixel by pixel, also on your device.

  • BasicINKOS 1, black and white, 30 images a day. Free.
  • PlusAdds INKOS 1.1 in six colours and 1024 px.
  • StudioAdds INKOS 1 Pro and INKOS 1.1 Pro for manga and illustration, without daily limits.

The models run in this browser: the page downloads their weights once (about 13 MB for INKOS 1 and 1.1, about 115 MB for each Pro model) and draws on your device, on the graphics processor when the browser offers WebGPU. Nothing you type is sent to a model server. Training happens elsewhere; the numbers below are the real training record of the weights served here.

Your plan decides which models you can use, the largest size, and how many images and projects you can have. Billing is handled by Clerk.
